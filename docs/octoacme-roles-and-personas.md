# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers lead technical execution, team health, and delivery capacity. They translate the product and project plan into a manageable engineering roadmap and keep the team aligned on dependencies, quality, and throughput.

### Responsibilities
- Organize engineering delivery across squads or teams
- Balance workload, capacity, and priorities with product and project leads
- Remove technical blockers and support dependency management
- Partner with developers on architecture, quality, and delivery trade-offs
- Coach team practices for sustainable execution and continuous improvement

### Goals
- Deliver predictable engineering execution
- Improve team health, delivery throughput, and technical quality
- Reduce delivery friction caused by unclear ownership or dependencies

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and team capacity reviews
- Technical dependency updates and staffing discussions
- Weekly coordination with PMs and Product Managers on delivery risk

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Project Managers on schedules, risks, and capacity planning
- Collaborates with Product Managers on roadmap feasibility and sequencing
- Supports Developers by clarifying priorities, removing blockers, and aligning technical decisions
- Escalates cross-team dependencies and delivery constraints to stakeholders when needed

---

## Security and Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads protect the organization and customers by ensuring relevant controls, policies, and risk reviews are built into delivery from the start.

### Responsibilities
- Review security, privacy, and compliance requirements during planning and execution
- Assess risks and recommend controls or mitigations for the project
- Validate that work meets required standards before release
- Coordinate security reviews, approvals, and remediation steps when issues are identified
- Ensure stakeholders understand important risk trade-offs and dependencies

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk throughout the project lifecycle
- Embed governance into delivery rather than treating it as a late-stage gate
- Improve trust and operational readiness for production use

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, risk assessments, and compliance checkpoints
- Escalations for high-risk issues or policy exceptions
- Coordination with PMs, Engineering Managers, and release stakeholders

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Product Managers to ensure requirements and priorities reflect security and compliance constraints
- Partners with Engineering Managers to integrate controls into technical delivery plans
- Advises Developers on secure design, testing, and remediation work
- Coordinates with Project Managers on timing, dependencies, and risk communication before release

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads define validation strategy and release-readiness checks so teams can confidently deliver features that meet acceptance criteria and operational expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and acceptance criteria coverage
- Coordinate unit, integration, and end-to-end validation approaches
- Establish defect triage and prioritization with engineering and product partners
- Validate release readiness and support rollout confidence checks
- Identify quality risks early and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Improve confidence in the quality and stability of each release
- Reduce defects escaping to production
- Align validation efforts with customer impact and business priorities

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage and quality risk discussions
- Coordination with Product Managers, Developers, and Release Managers

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Collaborates with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and user-impact validation
- Works with Developers to define test coverage and defect ownership
- Supports Project Managers with status updates and release gating information
- Coordinates with Security and Compliance Leads when compliance sensitivity or risk verification is required

---

## Release Managers / Delivery Coordinators

### Role Summary
Release Managers help plan and coordinate production rollouts, communication, and rollback readiness so releases are safe, timely, and transparent.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare and manage release schedules, deployment windows, and rollback readiness
- Coordinate with engineering, QA, and stakeholders on launch readiness
- Maintain release notes, communication plans, and stakeholder notifications
- Track post-deploy verification and issue response for launch activities
- Support incident triage when deployment problems require rapid action

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and operational disruption
- Ensure smooth communication before, during, and after releases
- Improve predictability for key delivery milestones

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and launch checklists
- Post-deploy reviews and stakeholder communications
- Escalation coordination for major defects or rollback decisions

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Project Managers on release timing, dependencies, and stakeholder communication
- Partners with Engineering Managers and Developers on deployment readiness and rollback plans
- Collaborates with QA / Test Leads on gate criteria and validation evidence
- Aligns with Product Managers on essential release messaging and customer impact updates

---

## Customer / User Advocates or Stakeholder Partners

### Role Summary
Customer / User Advocates represent the end-user perspective and operational interests of stakeholders, helping teams validate that the project delivers value and remains aligned with real-world needs.

### Responsibilities
- Capture user needs, adoption concerns, and stakeholder expectations
- Provide feedback on usability, customer impact, and value realization
- Help prioritize outcomes based on customer experience and business needs
- Support readiness checks for launch, training, and adoption activities
- Surface risks related to partner, support, or stakeholder readiness

### Goals
- Ensure projects remain focused on customer value and measurable outcomes
- Strengthen adoption and stakeholder confidence
- Close the gap between delivery decisions and user impact

### Typical Communication
- User feedback sessions, stakeholder reviews, and roadmap discussions
- Product validation, launch readiness, and adoption check-ins
- Escalation of customer-impacting risks or blockers to PM and Product leads

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Partners with Product Managers on needs, requirements, and success metrics
- Provides input to Project Managers and Engineering Managers on priorities, release readiness, and user impact
- Helps Product and Engineering teams translate feedback into actionable improvements
- Supports QA and Release work by validating customer-critical scenarios and communication needs

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The expanded set helps teams clarify ownership, improve accountability, and support better cross-functional decision-making throughout the project lifecycle.
